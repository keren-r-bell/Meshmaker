# The Color Palette

The **Color Palette** is designed to make quick alterations to the colors after they've been created between the mesh's existing points.  

---
  
-   On the Sides of the palette are swatches to change the **Hue**.  
-   The two stacked swatches on the left change the **Brightness**,  
-   and the two staccked swatches on the right change the **Saturation**.  
  
@InteractiveView(name: "Palette")  

Go ahead and give it a try!  

You can **drag** the colors from the swatches to points on the Mesh.   

---


***Q:*** *Why can't I input specific color values?*

**A:** Currently, I don't plan to allow adding specific hex values (or any precise values) via text input. Meshmaker is meant to quickly jam together a visual asset, not precise brand-asset work, as it's not indepth or persistent enough to facilitate this use.  

Seek alternative solutions to Meshmaker if you need maximum precision for your mesh gradients.
